Inflation — Crown Dependencies

Inflation rates for the Isle of Man, Jersey, Guernsey and the UK. Annual data from 1950 (Jersey & Guernsey) and 1977 (IoM). Monthly IoM vs UK back to March 1977.

View
75 years of island inflation. Jersey and Guernsey go back to 1950. IoM starts 1977 — the island's General Index of Retail Prices was only formally published from that year. All three islands tracked UK inflation broadly through the 1970s oil shocks, then diverged increasingly from the 1990s as their financial sectors created different local price dynamics. The 2022–23 spike was universal — but IoM peaked a year later due to the way its CPI is calculated (financial year averages).
Series notes: IoM: RPI (Dec annual %, General Index of Retail Prices, base Jan 2000=100) 1977–2008; CPI (financial year average Apr–Mar, base Jan 2008=100) 2009+. Dashed line = RPI. Jersey: annual average RPI index (base Jun 2000=100) → derived annual %. Guernsey: RPI December-quarter annual %. UK: RPI (Dec annual %) used as benchmark for pre-2009 comparisons; CPIH (Dec annual %) for 2009+ as ONS preferred measure. IoM series break: RPI uses Carli formula; CPI uses Jevons. FY lag means IoM CPI peak appears 2023 vs UK peak 2022.

Benchmark: UK RPI (Dec annual %) used as benchmark for all years pre-2009. UK CPIH (Dec annual %) used for 2009+ comparisons. This switch is necessary because CPIH is the ONS preferred modern measure but doesn't exist as a clean series pre-2009. Jersey and Guernsey RPI includes mortgage interest payments; UK benchmarks do not — the comparison is directionally valid but not methodology-identical. IoM RPI (pre-2009) is the closest like-for-like to UK RPI.

Methodology note: Jersey and Guernsey housing groups include mortgage interest payments (MIP) — they move with BoE base rate changes. IoM Group 04 and UK Division 04 exclude MIP. The 2022–23 housing spikes in Jersey (+24pp) and Guernsey (+12pp) are primarily mortgage rate effects, not structural housing costs. The ex-housing comparison is the methodologically cleaner signal.
What's driving the island premium? The two charts show separately: how island housing costs have diverged from UK housing costs, and how island prices (excluding housing) have diverged from UK prices excluding housing. The ex-housing differential is the most stable signal of the persistent island cost-of-living premium — driven by import costs, transport, and smaller markets.
Method: Housing differential = island housing rate − UK CPI Div04 rate (ONS D7GB). Ex-housing differential = island ex-housing rate − UK ex-Div04 rate. Island ex-housing derived as: (all_items − w×h) / (1−w) where w = housing group weight. UK ex-Div04 derived identically using ONS annual weights (CHZU). IoM weight: 154.9/1000 (2008 basket, fixed). Jersey weights: 238–252/1000 (basket reviews). Guernsey weights: 228–235/1000 (basket reviews). Two separate Y-axes — housing swings ±25pp, ex-housing ±5pp; shared axis would crush the ex-housing signal.

Isle of Man — Retail Prices Index 1977–2008 Official
Table 4.2, Isle of Man Digest of Economic and Social Statistics 2009. Publisher: Treasury, Economic Affairs Division. Monthly 12-month % change per calendar month, plus annual average. Index base: March 1976=100 (rebased January 2000=100 in later publications). Covers all private households exc. top 3-4% income group and social assistance recipients. 1977 starts March (first full quarter available).
Isle of Man — Consumer Price Index 2008–2025 Official
Statistics IoM Historic Data Sets December 2025 (PDF, extracted via Tabula). Monthly all-items CPI index, base January 2008=100. Group 04 (Housing, water, electricity, gas & other fuels) monthly index available 2008–2025, used for decomposition from January 2013 (where tabula extraction is complete). Financial year averages (April–March) used for annual headline comparisons; year label = calendar year of March year-end. CPI first introduced 2008 — the RPI (General Index of Retail Prices) was the island's price measure before that.

Download: gov.im blocks automated access. PDF must be downloaded manually from: gov.im/inflation (gov.im/media/1391380/december-2025-historic-datasets.pdf)
Jersey — Retail Prices Index Official
Statistics Jersey Open Data. Licence: Open Government Licence Jersey v1.0. Two series used: (1) RPI annual average index (base June 2000=100), 1949–2024 — annual % derived as (index_n / index_{n-1}) − 1. (2) RPI group indices December quarter, base June 2000=100, March 2000–March 2026 — used for housing group (includes MIP) and RPIX (excludes MIP). opendata.gov.je
Guernsey — Retail Prices Index Official
States of Guernsey Data and Analysis. Supplementary and Historical Inflation Data to Q1 2026 (Excel). Official quarterly series from June 1949. RPI all-items, RPIX (excludes MIP), housing group (includes MIP, rents, TRP, rates, water, waste) from December 2005, RPIX housing from December 2015. gov.gg/rpi
UK Official
RPI December annual rate 1960–2025: ONS historical series. CPIH December annual rate 2009–2026: ONS monthly bulletin. CPI all-items monthly 1989–2026: ONS series D7G7. CPI Division 04 (Housing, water, electricity, gas) monthly annual rate 1989–2026: ONS series D7GB. UK Division 04 basket weights (parts per 1000): ONS CHZU, annual.

UK CPIH used as benchmark from 2009 as the ONS preferred headline measure. UK RPI used for pre-2009 comparisons and as the like-for-like benchmark against island RPI measures.
Coverage limits
