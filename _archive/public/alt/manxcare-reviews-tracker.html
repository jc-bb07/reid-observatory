Avoidable mortality — age-standardised rate per 100,000, under 75s · OECD/Eurostat cause list · ESP2013
Avoidable mortality counts deaths under 75 from conditions where timely treatment or prevention should have made a difference. A lower rate is better — IoM outperforms England but trails its Crown Dependency peers by ~30%.
All figures use the OECD/Eurostat 2019 joint cause list, ESP2013 age-standardisation, per 100,000 under-75s. Single-year rates on small populations are volatile — Guernsey uses a 3-year rolling average for this reason. Hover the score on any card to see the trend over time.
