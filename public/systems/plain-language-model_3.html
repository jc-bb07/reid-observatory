Where does the Island's money actually go?

In 2011, a discussion paper argued the Island's tax system creates a hidden pressure that pushes up local prices and squeezes ordinary residents — unless some of the value flowing through the international-money sector is used deliberately. Move the sliders below to see how that plays out.

In plain English
This is a simplified public version of a full interactive economic model — the same equations, fewer dials. It is not a forecast: it is a first-pass, openly-flagged approximation, most useful for seeing the shape of trade-offs rather than exact numbers. Checked against real published wages, house prices and government spending, 2016–2025: wages track closely, house prices and spending are off by less than a percentage point a year — see the full technical model for the real-data overlay and the numbers behind it. Read the full communication paper or explore the technical model (with every lever exposed) for the underlying data, sources and caveats.
