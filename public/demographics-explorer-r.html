Dependency ratios
What is a dependency ratio? It measures how many retired people exist for every 100 working-age people. Isle of Man and Guernsey now exceed the UK; Jersey remains slightly below.

IoM & Guernsey exceed UK
The key pattern: IoM's ratio was higher than the UK through the 1970s–80s, fell sharply as the finance sector boom attracted working-age incomers, and is now climbing again as that cohort approaches retirement.
