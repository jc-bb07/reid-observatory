What "monthly" means here: Each data point is the 12-month rolling rate — prices in that month vs the same month a year earlier. What the monthly view reveals that annual charts hide: turning points within a year. IoM CPI peaked at 10.8% in July 2022; UK CPI peaked at 11.1% in October 2022 — a three-month lag. The IoM also fell faster: by December 2022 IoM was at 7.4% while UK was still 10.5%.

Series break 2008→2009: IoM switched from RPI (base Jan 2000=100) to CPI (base Jan 2008=100). Different basket and formula. The joined line bridges the gap visually but the methodological shift is real — use the "RPI only" or "CPI only" modes for pure-series work.

IoM Housing (Group 04): "Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels" — not purely housing. Available Jan 2013–Dec 2025. Plotted as YoY% derived from the monthly index.
