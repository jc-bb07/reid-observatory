09 · Care tiers — primary, secondary, tertiary: how care flows on an island
Public lensHealthcare comes in three tiers: primary (your GP, dentist, pharmacist — the front door), secondary (the island hospital), and tertiary (specialist care the island can't provide, which means a plane or a ferry). Pick your island below to see all three tiers — what works, what's queued, and what flies.
Politician lensEvery tier has a distinct failure mode you can probe: primary care fails by price or queue; secondary by flow and staffing (never just beds — see Gent); tertiary by weather, contracts and invisible costs. The tier cards carry the numbers; dashboards 03–08 carry the question generators.
Management lensSix natural experiments in the same physics. The pattern that travels: keep care local where volume allows (WI: 93% of unscheduled care; Shetland: MRI repatriation), manage the front door deliberately (Jersey's subsidy laddering), and publish enough that your consultants can benchmark themselves (Guernsey MSG KPI set, Manx Care specialty waits).
