England & Wales — ONS

  • METRIC: ASMR per 100,000 persons, 2013 European Standard Population, aged 10+.
  • COVERAGE: 1981–2024. ICD-9 before 1993; ICD-10 from 1993.
  • ⚠ BREAK 26 July 2018: Standard of proof lowered from criminal ("beyond all reasonable doubt") to civil ("balance of probabilities"). Significant upward shift in registered suicides. Series NOT directly comparable across this break. Chart shows pre/post as separate dashed segments.
  • Figures are for registered year, not year of occurrence. Inquest delays mean registration lags death by months to years.
  • Scotland (≈13–14/100k) and Northern Ireland (≈12–14/100k) excluded here but have consistently higher rates than E&W.

Isle of Man — IOM Public Health Directorate

  • PRIMARY METRIC (chart lines): 3-year rolling ASMR per 100,000 persons, 2013 European Standard Population — comparable to ONS and Guernsey. From IOM Mortality Report Figure 29 and FOI responses.
  • SECONDARY (bars): Annual registration counts from Figure 28, IOM Mortality Report 2022 (ICD-10 X60-X84 + Y10-Y34). 2023 confirmed separately (FOI ref. 4081089).
  • ⚠ DEFINITION DISCREPANCY across IOM sources: Figure 28 / Figure 29 use ICD-10 X60-X84 plus Y10-Y34 (undetermined intent). Several FOIs use confirmed suicide verdicts (X60-X84) only, giving counts consistently 2–3 lower per year. This is a material difference given the small absolute numbers.
  • ⚠ REGISTRATION vs. OCCURRENCE LAG: The Figure 28 series shows registration-year counts. Inquest delays on the IOM can be 12–18 months. Year-of-registration and year-of-death counts diverge significantly in some years (e.g. 2020 registrations=24; police year-of-death count=19).
  • ⚠ SMALL NUMBERS: Population ~85,000. One death shifts the crude rate by ~1.2/100k; annual counts range 2–24. IOM Public Health explicitly recommends 3-year rolling ASMR for trend analysis.
  • 3yr rolling ASMR values confirmed from FOI sources: 2014–16=7.8, 2015–17=10.2, 2016–18=10.7 (FOI ref. 1228481, DHSC). 2020–22=17.8 (IOM Mortality Report 2022 text). Values for other rolling windows read from Figure 29 chart — treat as approximate (±1–2 units).
  • Confirmed from text of IOM Mortality Report 2022: "For the 2020–22 period, the age-standardised mortality rate for deaths by suicide on the Isle of Man was 17.8 deaths per 100,000. This rate was higher than the England rate (10.3 per 100,000)."

⚠ Re-identification risk — IOM Constabulary data (FOI 3968233)

This FOI response provides annual death counts broken down by both year of death × sex × 5-category age group. With 12 deaths in 2023 all recorded as male, and only 2 deaths in 2024 (partial year), individual cells contain counts of 1–4 persons in a population of ~85,000. Cross-referencing against other local knowledge (e.g. known cases reported in Manx press) could plausibly identify specific individuals or families. This data has been released publicly under FOI but should not be reproduced in granular form in outputs intended for a general audience. The age/sex breakdown is therefore not charted here.

Guernsey — Public Health Intelligence Unit

  • METRIC: 3-year rolling ASMR per 100,000, 2013 European Standard Population. Plotted at midpoint year. Includes Alderney.
  • CONFIRMED DATA POINTS: 2013–15 avg = 7.0/100k; 2019–21 avg = 15.0/100k (Guernsey Mortality Trends 2021, Aug 2023). All other points interpolated — do not cite.
  • ⚠ EXTREME VOLATILITY: Annual counts 1–11, average 5.2/yr (2002–2021). 2021 spike of 11 deaths substantially drives the 2019–21 average.
  • Guernsey Mortality Trends 2024 (covering 2022–2024) published; specific suicide ASMR figures not yet obtained.

⚖ Comparability across all jurisdictions

Metric alignment: The IOM 3-year rolling ASMR (Figure 29), E&W ASMR (ONS), and Guernsey ASMR all use the 2013 European Standard Population — these three are directly methodologically comparable. The IOM annual registration counts are not age-standardised and should not be compared directly as rates.

Definition: All three reporting systems include "undetermined intent" (ICD-10 Y10-Y34) alongside confirmed suicides (X60-X84), which is standard practice. But some IOM FOI responses appear to use suicide-verdict only counts, running 20-30% lower. Always check the ICD-10 code range stated.

Registration lag: All jurisdictions report by year of registration not year of death. IOM inquest delays can run 12-18 months; the divergence between the 2020 registration count (24) and the police year-of-death count (19) for 2020 illustrates this clearly.
